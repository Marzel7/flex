# EB0.4E Operational-Family Corpus

EB0.4E groups verified EB0.4D evidence by explicit primary role. It preserves every operation, fact, nomination, source, shared feature, authority state, quality/completeness state and conflict while exposing only counts, lineage digests and exact replay.

The corpus is descriptive nomination evidence. It does not calculate profiles, rates, rankings, scores, policy, profitability, cashflow, or operator identity/attribution.
