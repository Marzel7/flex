## 🔍 Key Concepts Quick Reference

### Risk Levels
- 🟢 **LOW** (<30 score): Independent, no coordination detected
- 🟡 **MEDIUM** (30-49 score): Some coordination detected
- 🟠 **HIGH** (50-69 score): Clear coordination network
- 🔴 **CRITICAL** (≥70 score): Professional operation, immediate alert

### Coordination Patterns
1. **INDEPENDENT_CREATOR** - No coordination, LOW risk
2. **SOME_COORDINATION** - 1 treasury reuses, MEDIUM risk
3. **HIDDEN_COORDINATION** ⭐ - No reuse but Level 2 connected, MEDIUM risk
4. **NESTED_COORDINATION** - 1 treasury reuses + Level 2, MEDIUM risk
5. **COORDINATED_GROUP** - 2+ treasuries reuse, HIGH risk
6. **MULTI_LEVEL_COORDINATED_GROUP** - 2+ treasuries + Level 2, HIGH risk
7. **HIGHLY_COORDINATED_GROUP** - 5+ reuse or strong Level 2, CRITICAL risk
8. (The 8th is a subset/combination)

### Scoring Formula
```
Combined Score = Base + (Level2 × 0.3)

Base (Level 1):     10 / 35 / 60 / 80
Level 2:            0-100
Weighting:          70% Level 1, 30% Level 2
```

## 📞 Support & Questions

### Common Questions

**Q: What's the difference between Level 1 and Level 2?**
A: Level 1 detects when a treasury funds multiple creators (obvious coordination). Level 2 detects when a treasury is funded by a hub (hidden coordination). Together they catch both obvious AND sophisticated operations.

**Q: How fast are the alerts?**
A: ~2-3 seconds from token detection to alert display. End-to-end is ~5-8 seconds.

**Q: Can I use this offline?**
A: Yes, after initial database population. All analysis is local.

**Q: What's HIDDEN_COORDINATION?**
A: Treasury appears independent (Level 1 clean) but is funded by a hub (Level 2 connected). Catches sophisticated operations where reuse is deliberately hidden at the funding chain level.
