# EB1.3D Immutable Planning-Proposal Manifests

EB1.3D seals only the provenance-bearing verified projection produced by EB1.3C. Each manifest binds the exact EB1.1H bundle digest and authoritative requirement projection, manifest, and corpus digests; the EB1.2A review-history identity; EB1.3A contract and proposal-history identity; and the EB1.3C adapter version.

The manifest preserves every deterministic proposal record, alternative, assumption, authority lane, cohort/window scope, sequence, rationale, and supersession link. It records exact counts and replay identity without reviewing, selecting, ranking, acquiring, or fulfilling anything.

Every manifest remains `NON_EXECUTABLE_PLANNING_PROPOSAL`, with both planning and execution authority explicitly false. Qualification uses frozen and ephemeral fixtures only and makes no runtime or production compatibility claim.
