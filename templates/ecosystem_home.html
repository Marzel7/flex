Ecosystem Intelligence

One place to understand ecosystem quality, structure, expectancy, and capital-allocation context.

Why should I care?

Use Ecosystem Intelligence to decide whether a creator, network, funder, or farm structure has produced quality before, how coordinated it is, and whether our actual strategy has ever earned money there. The structure lenses below preserve all graph richness without making the graph implementation the product.

Two ledgers, kept separate: Historical Performance = observed ecosystem outcomes. Simulation PnL = actual paper-traded portfolio equity.
Canonical entities

Creators

Launch history, historical outcomes, runner production, and the bridge into creator dossiers.

Ecosystems

Network-level quality, risk composition, historical outcomes, and simulation coverage.

Funders

Expectancy attribution for capital sources and the creators they historically support.

Farm Structures

Cluster output, token history, and concentration patterns across detected farms.
Structure lenses

Overview

Joined ecosystem context across released networks, farms, coordinators, and coverage.

Coordinators

Wallets that organize multiple creators and concentrate operator influence.

Shared Funding

Overlap-driven coordination evidence across funders and creator groups.

Farm Structure

Detected farm clusters, member roles, and repeated launch patterns.

Graph Evidence

Raw transfer-index diagnostics and analyzer freshness when deeper proof is needed.

Released Networks

Legacy network index retained as an explorer view while consolidation proceeds.
