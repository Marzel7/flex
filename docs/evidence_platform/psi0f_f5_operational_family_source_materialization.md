# PSI0F-F5 immutable operational-family source materialization

PSI0F-F5 is a pure fixture-only contract that produces canonical
`psi0f-f1.logical-source.v1` bytes from caller-injected synthetic evidence
summaries. It performs no file, database, network, service, configuration, or
clock access.

The contract binds an explicitly ordered `PLATFORM_OPERATION_ID` cohort to
immutable EP3 evaluation identities and exact EB0.4C runtime projections. EP4
discovery candidates may supply reference identities and populations, but their
lifecycle never grants nomination authority. Every group requires a separate
immutable disposition that explicitly states `PROPOSED` or `SUPPORTED`, binds
the complete candidate identity, preserves exact member order, and carries
false identity, attribution, policy, ranking, integration, deployment, and
activation authority.

`SUPPORTED` dispositions require proven candidates, no missing or contradictory
evidence, complete non-conflicting runtime projections, and mechanism plus
temporal evidence. All inputs use exact schemas. Cohort, evaluation, runtime,
candidate, review, vocabulary, lineage, accounting, component-digest, and
authority drift fail closed.

The output is replay-verified through the unchanged PSI0F-F1 source contract.
PSI0F-F5 does not read real EP3/EP4 stores, write a source file, invoke the F1
rematerializer, publish an EB0.4H bundle, apply a PSI0F adapter, identify an
operator, or authorize production use.
