DATABASE:

Table: funder_outgoing_transfers
Columns:
  - funder_address (who sent)
  - recipient_address (who received)
  - amount_sol (how much)
  - transaction_signature (proof)
  - block_time (when)
  - recipient_type (cex/infra/unknown)
  - is_cex (1 or 0)
  - cex_exchange (Binance, MEXC, etc.)
  - cex_type (Hot Wallet, Cold Wallet, etc.)

Indexes:
  - idx_funder_outgoing (fast funder lookups)
  - idx_recipient_outgoing (fast recipient lookups)
  - idx_recipient_type (fast classification lookups)
