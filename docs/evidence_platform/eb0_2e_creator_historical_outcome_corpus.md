# EB0.2E deterministic per-creator outcome corpora

EB0.2E verifies EB0.2D manifests and groups their immutable facts by the
explicit creator identity already carried by each fact. Every distinct mint,
cohort boundary, horizon, threshold, outcome state, quality/completeness state,
source, and fact identity remains intact. Exact fact replay is deduplicated by
fact identity while all contributing manifest digests remain bound as lineage.

Each corpus exposes only fact, mint, eligible-denominator, UNKNOWN,
NOT_OBSERVED, conflict, outcome, quality, and completeness counts. Its digest
binds the complete ordered facts and manifest lineage. Replay reassembles all
corpora from the verified manifests and requires exact equality.

The corpus deliberately contains no rates, profiles, trends, rankings, scores,
policy decisions, profit/cashflow interpretation, or operator attribution.
Live sources, providers, GMGN, and activation remain outside EB0.2E.
