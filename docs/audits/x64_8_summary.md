# X64.8 Summary

Creator Identity is now a first-class, mutually exclusive Discovery stage between Behaviour and Topology.

## Reused intelligence

Creator address, launch count/history, CREATE/migration timestamps and signatures come from `token_analysis`. Creator age reuses existing birth selection and timing normalization. Transaction depth and first/last transaction come from `creator_tx_ledger`. Existing creator indexes and Operational Intelligence caching are reused.

## Newly derived metrics

- one Creator Identity per launch;
- prior-launch gap;
- Disposable Creator Score with evidence coverage;
- identity and score distributions for the selected Discovery window.

No phase required additional RPC. No index, schema, attribution, registry, Operator, Operation or WATCHTOWER scoring change was required.

Fresh and Single-use filters isolate disposable-style creator cohorts before topology analysis. Repeat, Returning and Dormant categories prevent established or reactivated creators with rapid migration from being conflated with fresh WATCHTOWER-style launches. This materially improves analyst reduction while remaining independent of operational attribution.

## Current 24-hour population

Of 625 launches: 40 Fresh (6.4%), 78 Single-use (12.5%), 493 Repeat (78.9%), 4 Returning (0.6%), 2 Dormant / Reactivated (0.3%), and 8 Unknown (1.3%). The 88-launch Quick Birth -> Migration cohort splits into 40 Fresh and 48 Repeat. Fresh identity reduces that behavioral working set by 54.5% before topology analysis, demonstrating material discrimination against rapid but established launchers.

No canonically attributed WATCHTOWER launch is present in the current endpoint's 24-hour attribution population, so a WATCHTOWER-specific recall percentage cannot honestly be reported from this live window. The identity improvement is established as cohort reduction, not attribution proof.

No further identity category is recommended until reliable balance snapshots or SPL-history coverage exist. Those facts should be added as evidence sources first, not inferred from incomplete transfers.
