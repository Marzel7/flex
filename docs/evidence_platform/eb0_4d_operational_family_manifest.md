# EB0.4D Operational-Family Manifest

EB0.4D seals canonical EB0.4A facts and nominations produced through EB0.4C. The immutable manifest binds contract and adapter versions, exact input/fact/nomination digests, authority-state, role, quality, completeness and conflict counts, and its own digest.

Replay reprojects every fact and nomination and rejects missing support, duplicates, mutation, version drift or authority promotion. It remains non-authoritative nomination evidence: it exposes no operator identity, profile, rank, score, policy, profitability or cashflow semantics.
