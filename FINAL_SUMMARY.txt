================================================================================
                      WHAT IS PUMPSWAP?
================================================================================

PumpSwap = Raydium V4 pools where PumpFun tokens migrate after their bonding
curve completes.

DETECTION:
  A token is PumpSwap if it has TWO markers:
    ✓ bonding_curve field (was on PumpFun)
    ✓ raydium_pool field (migrated to Raydium V4)

BROADCAST:
  Each detected token gets:
    • is_pumpswap: True
    • pumpswap_badge: '🚀 PumpSwap'

DATABASE:
  All metadata stored in SQLite:
    • Creator authority
    • Bonding curve address
    • Migration timestamp
    • Social links (website, twitter, discord)
    • Pricing information
